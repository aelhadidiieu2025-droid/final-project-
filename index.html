Five ideas behind the rover

One morning on the farm, told in the words people use for artificial intelligence. Each idea shows up at a different hour.

06:00

Input

The rover leaves the barn and starts taking photos, about one every two meters. Data is just what the machine can sense.

06:40

Pattern

Each photo is compared with what the model learned from thousands of labelled leaves. It looks for yellow, spots, and curl.

07:30

Prediction

For every tree it makes a guess: healthy, thirsty, or spotted. A guess is not a fact.

07:50

Confidence

The report carries a percentage next to each guess. 91% sure is not the same as certain, and the farmer knows it.

08:30

Feedback

The farmer checks the flagged trees and says when the rover was wrong. That correction is how the next version gets better.

Times and numbers are from a concept scenario, not from a tested machine.
